Position: Senior Data Engineer, Enterprise Data Platform
Division: Group Data & Analytics
Location: Sydney CBD (3 days on-site)
Employment type: Permanent, full-time

About Meridian Bank

Meridian is one of Australia's tier-one financial institutions, serving over 8 million retail and business customers. Our Group Data & Analytics function is responsible for the secure, compliant, and reliable provision of data across the Bank, supporting regulatory reporting, risk management, customer analytics, and AI/ML use cases.

Role purpose

The Senior Data Engineer will be responsible for the design, development, and operation of enterprise-grade data pipelines on the Bank's strategic data platform (Snowflake on AWS, with dbt as the primary transformation framework). The successful candidate will work within a disciplined delivery framework, in close partnership with data stewards, data architects, security, and downstream consuming teams (risk, finance, marketing analytics).

Key responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain robust data ingestion and transformation pipelines feeding the Bank's certified data products
- Ensure all data pipelines meet the Bank's data quality, observability, and lineage standards, including coverage by appropriate controls and reconciliation processes
- Partner with Data Governance to ensure all data products comply with the Bank's Data Management Policy, including classification of sensitive customer data (PII) and adherence to data retention requirements
- Contribute to platform-level capabilities including metadata management, data lineage, and CI/CD frameworks
- Provide subject matter expertise to downstream teams during onboarding to the platform
- Participate in change advisory board (CAB) processes and contribute to design review forums
- Support incident management for production data pipelines, including root cause analysis and remediation
- Mentor mid-level engineers and contribute to the team's engineering standards

Essential criteria

- 7+ years of experience in data engineering roles, with at least 3 years in a large enterprise environment (financial services preferred)
- Demonstrated experience designing and operating production-grade pipelines on Snowflake and/or comparable cloud data warehouses
- Strong dbt experience, including dbt project structure, testing, documentation, and CI/CD practices
- Sound understanding of dimensional modeling and data warehousing principles (Kimball / Inmon)
- Demonstrated experience implementing data quality frameworks and observability tooling
- Familiarity with data governance principles, including data classification, lineage, and metadata management
- Proven track record of partnering with non-technical stakeholders to translate business requirements into well-modeled data products
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to produce design documentation suitable for architecture review

Desirable

- Experience with regulatory reporting workloads (APRA, AUSTRAC, BCBS239)
- Exposure to data mesh and data product operating models
- Experience with infrastructure-as-code (Terraform) in a regulated environment

Australian Citizenship or Permanent Residency required. The successful candidate will be required to undergo a National Police Check and credit check prior to commencement.
