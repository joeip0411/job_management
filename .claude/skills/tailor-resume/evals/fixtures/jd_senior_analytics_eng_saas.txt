Senior Analytics Engineer
Company: Cloudwave (B2B SaaS, ~600 ppl, Series D)
Location: Remote (Australia or NZ) — quarterly travel to Sydney HQ

Cloudwave is the operating system for facilities and field-services businesses. We're profitable, growing 60% YoY, and serve 4,000+ customers across ANZ, the US, and EMEA. Our data team sits inside the broader Platform org and partners closely with GTM, Finance, and Product.

The opportunity

We're hiring a Senior Analytics Engineer to join our 8-person data team (3 analytics engineers, 2 data engineers, 2 analysts, 1 manager). You'll be the most senior IC on the analytics engineering side and you'll set the standard for how we build and ship trusted data products to the rest of the business.

Today our stack is:

- Snowflake as the warehouse
- dbt Cloud for transformation (we have ~700 models and growing)
- Fivetran and a small set of internal Python ingestion services for sources
- Looker as the primary BI layer, with a homegrown semantic layer effort underway
- GitHub + dbt CI for our delivery workflow

What you'll do

- Own the modeling and curation of our core revenue, customer, and product analytics marts — these are the marts the exec team, finance, and the GTM org rely on every day
- Partner with stakeholders in Finance, Sales Ops, and Customer Success to translate ambiguous business questions into well-modeled, well-documented, well-tested data products
- Lead our semantic-layer initiative — we want to consolidate metric definitions so "MRR" means the same thing everywhere, and you'll drive that
- Raise the bar on dbt project hygiene — model conventions, testing standards, documentation, exposures, deprecation processes
- Mentor the two more junior analytics engineers; review their PRs and pair with them on harder modeling problems
- Partner with the data engineering team on contracts between raw/staging layers and your downstream marts

What we're looking for

- 5+ years working in analytics engineering, BI engineering, or a closely related discipline; meaningful time in a senior IC role
- Deep dbt expertise — not just writing models, but thinking about project structure, testing strategy, and how the whole thing scales past a few hundred models
- Strong SQL and dimensional modeling chops (Kimball-style or equivalent — we don't dogmatic about the school)
- A track record of building data products that real business stakeholders trust and use, not just dashboards
- Communication skills that let you explain a tricky modeling tradeoff to a non-technical exec without losing them
- Experience with a semantic / metrics layer (LookML, Cube, dbt Semantic Layer, MetricFlow) is a strong plus
- Bonus: SaaS-domain familiarity (ARR/MRR/NRR, sales funnel modeling, product analytics)

How we work

- Async-first, but with deliberate sync time
- "Strong opinions, weakly held" is a real value here — we'd rather you push back than nod along
- We invest in tooling. If you think dbt-checkpoint, SQLFluff, Elementary, or similar would help, we'll find the budget.
